Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Applies to applications, browser extensions and tools published by gmkbenjamin

1. Scope and Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how software applications, Chrome and browser extensions, utilities and web tools maintained by gmkbenjamin ("we", "our", or "the developer") handle information. Our software is built on a local-first model: we do not operate user-tracking servers, sell personal data, or monetize user activity through advertising.

Where an extension or application processes sensitive website or financial data on your device, it presents an in-product disclosure and requires your explicit acceptance before reading page data or making background network requests.

2. Information Processed by Our Apps and Extensions

Depending on the specific application or browser extension you install and the permissions it declares, our software may process the following categories of information locally on your device:

  • User preferences and configuration: Rules, alerts, thresholds, watched items, interface preferences, and local activity logs stored in your browser or device storage (such as chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.session).
  • Website content on declared hosts: For browser extensions that augment specific websites, the extension reads only the DOM elements, inputs, status indicators, and public identifiers (such as a public wallet address displayed on the page) necessary to provide the extension's single purpose on the declared host(s).
  • Financial or trading interface data (where applicable): For trading-companion extensions, visible position details, order ticket fields, margin, profit/loss (PnL), queue status, and public token balances displayed by the target website. Our extensions never read or collect payment card numbers, bank account details, private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or authentication cookies/tokens.
  • Local activity logs: Timestamps, action outcomes, errors, and the relevant page path on the target website so you can audit what the tool did on your device. Our extensions do not read or collect general web browsing history.

3. Network Connections and Third-Party Services

We do not run developer telemetry, analytics, crash-reporting, or data-collection servers for our browser extensions. Your local settings, rules, and activity logs are never transmitted to the developer.

When an application or extension fetches public data directly from third-party APIs or WebSocket feeds (such as public market prices, protocol summaries, or transaction queue status) to perform its disclosed features, those third-party providers receive your IP address, standard HTTPS/WSS connection metadata, and the public query parameters required for the request, governed by their own privacy policies.

All executable code (JavaScript, HTML, CSS) is bundled inside the application or extension package. Remote responses are parsed strictly as data and are never executed as code.

4. Local Storage, Security and Retention

Data used by our browser extensions is stored locally in your browser profile using standard platform APIs (chrome.storage.local for persistent settings, rules, snapshots, and recent activity logs, and chrome.storage.session or memory for temporary cross-tab coordination).

  • Access to local extension storage is governed by your browser and operating system profile permissions. Do not use shared or untrusted browser profiles when using extensions that display or process sensitive account or financial data.
  • You can stop all extension processing and background alarms at any time by disabling the extension in your browser's extension manager.
  • Uninstalling the extension or application permanently removes its local extension storage from your browser.

5. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

The use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We limit our use of user data strictly to providing and improving the single purpose of the specific extension you installed.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not allow human reading of user data by the developer, as user data remains on your device.

6. Product Annex: PaperManager for Papertrade (v0.1.0)

Single purpose: PaperManager for Papertrade is an independent Chrome extension that helps you manage and monitor Papertrade (exchange.papertrade.xyz) positions, queued executions, and watched entries, with related PAPER balance alerts. It is not affiliated with Papertrade or Hyperliquid.

First-use consent

After installation, PaperManager opens a setup screen. Before using it, you must click Accept and enable PaperManager to accept the financial risk & liability disclaimer and data-handling disclosure. Without acceptance, PaperManager does not read trading data, start public feeds, or run trading controls.

Information used on Papertrade

On https://exchange.papertrade.xyz/*, PaperManager reads visible position identifiers, coins, sides, leverage, size, margin, entry and mark prices, PnL, button states, ticket fields, pending queue details, the connected public wallet address shown by the site, and public PAPER balance/staking/protocol totals. It uses this data to evaluate your configured rules, verify the intended position or ticket, watch prices, coordinate tabs, press Papertrade's own buttons (Close, Retry, Cancel, side toggle, Place Trade), and display local status or notifications.

PaperManager never reads private keys, seed phrases, passwords, authentication cookies, wallet storage, or session keys, and never signs or directly calls Papertrade's private trading intent API.

Local storage and background checks

chrome.storage.local stores your consent receipt, settings and default rules, position overrides and recovery state, ticket defaults, watched orders, protection expectations (which expire after one hour), last public wallet, PAPER/protocol snapshots, and up to the latest 300 local activity entries. chrome.storage.session holds temporary cross-tab action claims and cancellation-helper state. PaperManager does not add application-level encryption to chrome.storage.local; your operating system and Chrome profile control access.

After consent, background chrome.alarms checks can run once per minute to read public PAPER holdings for the last stored public wallet even after Papertrade tabs close or automatic actions/alerts are toggled off. Disable the extension in Chrome to stop all background checks, or uninstall it to delete all stored records. Chrome notifications may display financial amounts on your screen.

Public network endpoints used by PaperManager

  • https://exchange.papertrade.xyz/state/user/live (sends the public wallet address, without HTTP credentials, to read public PAPER holdings) and https://exchange.papertrade.xyz/query/protocol/summary (reads public staking/supply totals).
  • https://exchange.papertrade.xyz/state/trading (reads public instrument parameters for indicative liquidation estimates while a trade ticket is in a visible tab; omits credentials and wallet address; held in memory only for up to 15 seconds).
  • wss://stream.papertrade.xyz/connection/websocket (or a Papertrade subdomain configured by the page: anonymous pressure:current queue subscription).
  • wss://api.hyperliquid.xyz/ws (subscribes to public l2Book market prices by coin symbol; never sends your wallet, positions, or activity history).
