Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Applies to the apps, browser extensions and tools published by SmartStay Access

1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy covers the apps, browser extensions and tools published by SmartStay Access ("we", "us"): Latch, ResetCalendar, Tailcat, Shell and PaperManager for Papertrade. Each product has a section below and a full policy that goes into detail. Where a product's own section is more specific, it applies.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not run analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking services, and our products include no third-party SDK that sends data to us or to anyone else. We do not sell, share or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or credit decisions. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give, correct or delete on request.

3. Data that stays on your devices

Our products keep what they need to work — settings, pairings, keys, saved connections and local history — on your own devices, using the platform's protected storage such as the iOS and macOS Keychain or your browser's extension storage. Some products can, at your choice, sync data to your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access, or export backups that you store yourself.

Deleting an iOS or macOS app removes its files and settings, but the system keeps an app's Keychain items after it is deleted. Each product explains how to remove those items first. Uninstalling a browser extension removes its local extension storage.

4. Connections our products make

Our products connect only where you direct them, or where a feature you use needs it: to your own paired devices, to servers you choose to connect to, and to the third-party services named in each product's section. Those services receive your IP address and standard connection details, and handle them under their own privacy policies. All program code ships inside the app or extension; data received from the network is never run as code.

5. Permissions

Each product asks for a system permission (such as camera, location, local network, Bluetooth, calendars or Face ID) only for the feature that needs it, explains why when it asks, and works without the permissions you decline, apart from that feature. You can change them at any time in your device's settings.

6. Children

Our products are not directed at children, and they collect no data from anyone.

7. Latch

Latch unlocks and controls your own Mac from your own iPhone or iPad. Pairing records, a random device identifier, lock and unlock history, optional security photos, authenticator accounts and settings stay on your devices. Requests travel only between your paired devices, over your local network, Bluetooth, or a private network you run yourself. The Mac app stores your Mac login password in its Keychain so it can type it at the lock screen when you ask. No data reaches us. Full Latch privacy policy

8. ResetCalendar

ResetCalendar shows your AI service quota and reset times on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. On the Mac, it uses the credentials you enter — or, for some services, a sign-in those tools already keep on your Mac — to request your usage directly from the services you enable, and reads their public status pages. Your phone holds no service credentials; it receives readings from your paired Mac and, if you turn it on, your own iCloud account. Calendar features manage only ResetCalendar's own events. No data reaches us. Full ResetCalendar privacy policy

9. Tailcat

Tailcat lets your iPhone or iPad reach your own devices — to open a terminal, browse and transfer files, and view web pages they serve. Saved connections and keys stay in the iOS Keychain on your device. What you send travels end-to-end encrypted between your devices, directly or through Tailscale's public relay servers, which help them find each other and cannot read the traffic. No data reaches us. Full Tailcat privacy policy

10. Shell

Shell is an SSH and Mosh terminal for iPhone and iPad. Hosts, keys, settings, history and snippets stay on your device (keys and passwords in the iOS Keychain), with optional sync of hosts to your own private iCloud account. Shell connects to the servers you choose and, unless you turn it off, downloads a public snippet library from GitHub. No data reaches us. Full Shell privacy policy

11. PaperManager for Papertrade

PaperManager is a Chrome extension that helps you manage and monitor Papertrade positions. It reads only the Papertrade pages it is declared for, after you accept its disclosure, stores its data in Chrome's local extension storage, and reads public market and protocol data. No data reaches us. Full PaperManager privacy policy

12. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

For our browser extensions, the use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: we use data only to provide the extension's single purpose, never sell or transfer it, never use it for advertising or creditworthiness, and never read it ourselves, because it stays on your device.

13. Changes and contact

We publish changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date. A product that needs your consent for a change will ask for it again in the product before the change applies.

Questions about privacy: dev@smartstayaccess.com. Please don't send passwords, keys, wallet details or financial screenshots.
