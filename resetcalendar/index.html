ResetCalendar Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Part of the SmartStay Access privacy policy

ResetCalendar shows how much of your AI service quota is left and when it resets, on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This policy covers all of those apps. ResetCalendar is published by SmartStay Access.

Summary

ResetCalendar collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK that sends data anywhere. Nothing is sent to the developer. Your Mac reads your usage directly from the AI services you choose, and shares it only with your own paired devices and, if you turn it on, your own private iCloud account.

Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from any ResetCalendar app: no usage data, no diagnostics, no crash reports and no identifiers.

On your Mac

The Mac app collects your usage. Every provider is off until you enable it.

  • Credentials for the services you enable. You can enter an API key, session cookie, account ID or service URL, which is stored in your Mac's Keychain and never sent to your phone. For some services the Mac can instead reuse a sign-in already on your Mac: the login files those tools keep in your home folder (for example ~/.codex/auth.json or ~/.claude), their settings folders, or the Claude Code sign-in in your Keychain, which macOS asks you to allow. These are read only to request your usage.
  • Requests to those services. The Mac sends each enabled service your credential, as that service requires, to read your quota, balance or spending. Those services see your IP address and receive the request under their own privacy policies. Azure OpenAI is checked with a small request that may be billed to your account.
  • Public status pages. While refreshing, the Mac reads public incident feeds for OpenAI, Claude, Cursor, GitHub, Augment and Factory, at most once every five minutes. No credentials are sent.
  • CodexBar. If you choose CodexBar as the source, the Mac reads readings from CodexBar on your Mac instead.
  • History. Readings are kept on the Mac for up to 90 days (at most 20,000), to show history and pace.
  • Calendar. If you enable Calendar sync, the Mac adds, updates and removes only ResetCalendar's own reset events in the calendar you choose. It uses calendar access only to find and manage those events; nothing from your calendars leaves your devices.

On iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

The phone app does not sign in to any AI service and holds no service credentials.

  • Readings from your Mac. After you pair by scanning a code from your Mac with the camera, the phone receives readings, a shortened history and service status from the Mac over an encrypted connection on your local network, or through a private network you run yourself such as Tailscale. The pairing code and connection token are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Apple Watch receives a summary of your quota from your iPhone. No credentials are sent to it.
  • Calendar Backup, when you turn it on, uses calendar access only to read ResetCalendar's own reset events, so the phone can show reset times while your Mac is unavailable.
  • Notifications are scheduled on the device. None come from a developer server.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, readings, alert rules and your Mac theme are stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. The developer cannot access it. Apple may notify your devices of changes. Credentials are never synced.

Diagnostics

Diagnostic logs stay on the device unless you choose to export and share them.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a pairing code. Nothing is recorded.
  • Calendars: only for the Calendar features above, when you turn them on.
  • Local network: to reach your paired Mac.
  • Notifications: reset and quota alerts you set up.

Keeping and deleting your data

Remove a provider on the Mac to delete its stored credential. Deleting an app removes its readings and settings, but iOS and macOS keep an app's Keychain items after it is deleted; unpair your devices and remove providers first to clear them. To delete iCloud data, turn off iCloud sync and remove ResetCalendar's data under System Settings or Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

ResetCalendar is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
