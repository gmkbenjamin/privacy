Shell Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Part of the SmartStay Access privacy policy

Shell is an SSH and Mosh terminal for iPhone and iPad. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive or collect any of your data.

Data on your device

Shell keeps your hosts, settings, command history, snippets and known host keys on your device, along with a copy of any terminal background image you choose. The system photo picker gives Shell only that image, not access to your library. SSH keys and saved passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain. If you turn on iCloud Sync (where available), your hosts are copied to your own private iCloud account, which the developer cannot access.

If you enable Settings → Diagnostics → Debug logs, Shell keeps up to 2 MiB of diagnostic events locally, replacing the oldest events as needed. These contain timestamps, app/session lifecycle events, connection outcomes, notification scheduling and numeric status details. They exclude terminal text, commands, host names, credentials and notification content. Logs are excluded from device backups and are not synced to iCloud or sent automatically. You can export them yourself, including the app and OS version, or delete them with Clear logs. Turning logging off keeps existing logs until you clear them.

Network connections

Shell connects only where you tell it to, or where a feature you use needs it:

  • Servers you connect to. Shell connects to the SSH, Mosh and SFTP servers you choose, and sends them what you type, your files and your credentials, as those protocols require.
  • GitHub. Shell downloads the public snippet library from GitHub, unless you turn off Settings → Snips → Public Snips Index. If you run mosh --install-static for a server that doesn't have Mosh, Shell asks first, then downloads mosh-server from GitHub and installs it on that server.
  • Websites you open. The code command opens vscode.dev or another address you give it, and browse opens the page you choose. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only for the FaceCam overlay you start with facecam. Video stays on your device and is not recorded.
  • Location: used only by the geo command, while Shell is running. Your location stays on your device.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: used to unlock Shell when Auto Lock is on. Shell never sees your biometric data.
  • Notifications: local notifications only, such as coding agent alerts and geo lock.
  • Local network: used to reach servers on your local network that you connect to.
